Tea Country

A cooler side of the island.

The central highlands bring a completely different rhythm: green slopes, wooden tea bungalows, scenic roads and rail lines, cool evenings and views that stretch across layers of mountain country.

Wake above the clouds.

Start with a warm cup of tea and a slow breakfast, then head out for a plantation walk, a waterfall stop or a scenic train journey. Tea Country is made for leaving gaps in the itinerary.
