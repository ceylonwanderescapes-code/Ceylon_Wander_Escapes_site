An island shaped by many eras

Past and present, woven together.

Sri Lanka's cultural landscape moves from ancient capitals and Buddhist monuments to Portuguese, Dutch and British-era architecture, village craft and rituals that still have a place in everyday life.

Beyond the checklist.

A heritage escape can be more than arriving, taking a photo and leaving. Slow down at a temple courtyard, walk old city walls in softer light, meet makers, taste regional food and leave space for the small details that make a place memorable.

Build your route

Three iconic chapters.

Combine one chapter or create a multi-stop cultural journey that moves through different regions and eras.

Ancient kingdom

Sigiriya

Rise above the surrounding forests, explore one of the island's defining archaeological landscapes and take in wide views from the rock.

Sacred city

Kandy

Pair temple visits with lake walks, traditional arts, gardens and the rich cultural atmosphere of Sri Lanka's hill country.

Colonial heritage

Galle Fort

Wander old ramparts and narrow lanes where architecture, cafés, galleries and ocean views meet inside a historic fortified town.
